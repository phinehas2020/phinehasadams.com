## Original content carried forward

Baseline: commit `3ad7c6b`, rendered separately during the review. Its homepage had eleven sections, including website inventory, background, process, photography, capabilities, and contact. The first redesign kept too little of that scope.

- Website inventory returns to Home and remains in the catalog. Available records are starting points for sale, not invented client case studies.
- The original approach and four-stage process inform Home and the service pages.
- The personal background, all eleven original capability subjects, and all six gallery photographs are retained on About.
- The public email, catalog links, policy pages, optional SMS consent, Studio, and revalidation endpoint remain available.
- Unsupported fixed delivery, Google ranking, and reply-time promises are omitted.

Navigation links to real pages, marks the current page, and stays visible on small screens. The sitemap covers all nine public page routes. Costs, timing, and ongoing support are discussed against the actual work.
